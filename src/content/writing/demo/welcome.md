Hello — welcome to my blog. It is where I write up the systems I actually
run, the day I run them:

- **Infrastructure as Code** — Terraform, Crossplane and the pipelines around them
- **Containerization** — Docker, Kubernetes, Operators, and deployment strategies
- **Cloud infrastructure** — AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, OCI
- **SRE & Platform Engineering** — monitoring, observability, developer experience, incident response
- **AI** — the coding agents and models that now work alongside the platform
- **Linux & NixOS** — system configuration and management
- **IoT** — from home automation to industrial applications

Every note is written from something I have set up, broken or measured, so the
judgements carry the reasons. To look around, start with the
[writing index](/writing); the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) delivers the notes as
they are written.
